The number still matters.

OpenCarrier.Network keeps the familiar North American–style NPA-NXX-XXXX number format. Each exchange owns an NPA (area code) and allocates numbers within it.

  • ❳
    Local dialing. Dial 867-1001 to reach a subscriber on your own exchange.
  • ❳
    Cross-exchange. Dial the full 440-867-1001 to reach another exchange.
  • ❳
    Reserved. 800 and 900 host network services, not subscribers.

Modern underneath.

OCN rebuilds the telephone infrastructure with Internet technology while keeping the usability of the traditional network.

✆

WebRTC voice

Encrypted calls (DTLS-SRTP) between softphones and browsers, with conference rooms mixed on the server.

ⓘ

Federation

Exchanges route to each other through the registry. No central operator controls the network.

✉

Text & image messages

1:1 messaging between OCN numbers, delivered across exchanges with an offline queue.

▪

Identity & mailboxes

Ed25519 kSIM identity with QR provisioning; visual voicemail and 800/900 services.

On the roadmap: SIP/ATA phone interop, PIN/DTMF voicemail boxes, and more exchanges joining the registry.

Run your own server.

OCN is built to be community-operated. You own your infrastructure, your area code, and your numbering space — an individual, a club, a campus, or a hosting provider.

  • 1.
    Run an ocnserver (Go) and point it at the registry.
  • 2.
    Claim an area code from the 200–999 pool and start hosting numbers.
  • 3.
    Provision softphones with QR / deep-link tokens from the admin panel.
